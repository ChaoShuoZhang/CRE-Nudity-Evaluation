# Data sources

- `i2p_sexual_931.csv`: the 931-prompt sexual subset of the I2P benchmark, preserving its prompts and evaluation seeds.
- `nudity_tr_200.csv`: the frozen 200-sample, human-selected same-concept text-retention split used in the paper.
- `coco_10k.csv`: the frozen first 10,000 rows of the project COCO-30K prompt file, including evaluation seeds.
- `anytext_1k.json`: the English 1K split of AnyText, used through the adapted text-to-image prompt described in the paper.

The repository includes prompts and annotations required for evaluation, but no generated images. Dataset terms remain governed by their original sources.
